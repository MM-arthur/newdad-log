# Newborn sleep / soothing / night waking (0-3 months)

> ⚠️ **For reference only, does not constitute medical advice**. Follow doctor's guidance.
> **Skill positioning**: Only record the framework (**total sleep, sleep signals, soothing, night waking**) and **common pitfalls**.
> Not recorded: specific lullaby music, white-noise audio, sleep sack brands.

---

## Basic framework (set expectations first)

### 0-3 month total sleep

| Age | Total sleep / day | Single sleep length | Wake-sleep interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-1 mo | 16-20 hours | 1-3 hours | 30-60 min |
| 1-3 mo | 15-18 hours | 1.5-3 hours | 1-1.5 hours |

> **Core principle**: Newborns **have no circadian rhythm** until ~2-3 months — **waking 3-5 times at night in 0-1 month is completely normal**

### 0-1 month sleep signals (5 types)

- 🟡 Fixed gaze / staring
- 🟡 Yawning
- 🟡 Sucking fingers / mouth
- 🟡 Fussiness / crying (late signal)
- 🟡 Ear pulling / eye rubbing

> **Soothing sweet spot**: First 3 signals = **easiest** to soothe; after 4th = **3x harder**

---

## 5-step soothing method (core)

1. **Watch for sleep signals** — start as soon as one of the 5 appears
2. **Reduce stimulation** — close curtains / lower noise / less interaction
3. **Build a routine** — diaper change → burp → gentle pat → humming (3-4 step fixed sequence)
4. **Swaddle** (0-2 mo) — mimics womb, **reduces startle reflex**
5. **White noise + gentle patting** — continue until baby is **fully asleep** before putting down

> **Key**: Put baby down only when **deep asleep** (eyes still, breathing smooth) — **putting down before fully asleep = guaranteed wake-up**

---

## 5 red flags (sleep abnormal signals)

- 🟥 0-1 month **total sleep < 12 hours** (too little)
- 🟥 **Each sleep < 30 min** and **can't go back to sleep** (fragmented sleep)
- 🟥 Persistent **restless sleep** (waking every 5-10 min)
- 🟥 **Breathing pauses > 20 sec** or **face turning blue** during sleep (SIDS / suffocation alert)
- 🟥 **3 months still day/night reversed** (sleeping all day, awake all night)

---

## 5 common myths

- ❌ **"Don't sleep in the day, then sleep well at night"** — **WRONG!** 0-3 month must sleep enough during day; **won't** cause night insomnia
- ❌ **"Feed whenever baby cries"** — night waking has many causes (hungry / hot / cold / gas / startle) — **assess first**
- ❌ **"Rock to sleep / feed to sleep"** — works short-term, **creates long-term dependency** (harder to break at 3-6 mo)
- ❌ **"Use night light"** — affects melatonin secretion; **keep dark as much as possible** (OK in 0-1 mo, change by 2-3 mo)
- ❌ **"Co-sleep with parents"** — **increases SIDS risk**; AAP recommends **same room, separate bed**

---

## 5-step night-waking response (0-3 month)

1. **Don't pick up immediately** — observe 1-2 min, baby may **self-soothe back to sleep** (this is the key to sleeping through by 2-3 mo)
2. **Gentle pat + shush** — no lights, no talking, no picking up
3. **No self-soothing in 3 min** — then pick up, change diaper / feed
4. **No chatting while feeding** — feed then put down, **build "night = sleep" association**
5. **Put down in 5-10 min** — even if not fully asleep, **don't hold while sleeping**

---

## 5-step bedtime routine (fixed 4 steps)

1. **Bath** (optional) — within 5 min
2. **Diaper change + sleep sack** — gentle movements
3. **Feed** (last feed can be slightly more) — but **don't overfeed to prevent spit-up**
4. **Burp + gentle pat + humming** — 5-10 min until fully asleep

> **Same sequence every night** — even when traveling; baby **forms expectation within 2 weeks**

---

## 5 environment elements (safe sleep)

1. **On back** — AAP explicit, **back-sleeping is safest** (prevents SIDS)
2. **Firm mattress** — not too soft (prevents suffocation)
3. **No pillows / blankets / stuffed toys** — **only the baby in the crib** for 0-1 month
4. **Temperature 24-26°C** — align with the temperature guide
5. **Same room, separate bed** — baby's crib next to parents' bed

---

## 快速自查

- [ ] 0-1 mo total sleep 16-20h / 3-5 night wakings = **completely normal**
- [ ] Missed soothing within 30 min of sleep signal = easy over-tiredness
- [ ] Sleep < 12h / each < 30 min = **see doctor for evaluation**
- [ ] Breathing pause / blue face = **see doctor immediately**
- [ ] Don't use "no day sleep" to get "good night sleep"
