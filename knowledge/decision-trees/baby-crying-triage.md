# Newborn crying triage (0-3 months)

> ⚠️ **For reference only, does not constitute medical advice**. Follow doctor's guidance.
> **Skill positioning**: A **triage framework** for the top 7 crying causes in 0-3 month newborns — most are normal physiology, but a few need urgent care.
> Not recorded: specific colic drops brands, anti-crying products.

---

## 5-step triage: is the crying urgent?

Before hunting for "the cause", **first rule out urgent situations**:

1. **Breathing**: smooth? nasal flaring? blue lips? → emergency
2. **Skin color**: pale / mottled / yellow? → if abnormal, urgent
3. **Body temperature**: ≥ 38°C? cold limbs? → see doctor
4. **Muscle tone**: floppy / limp / can't be awakened? → emergency
5. **Crying pattern**: high-pitched scream / weak moan / silent? → see doctor

→ Any of above = **see doctor immediately**, don't try to soothe at home

---

## 7 most common causes (90%+ of crying falls in this list)

| # | Cause | Time pattern | 快速自查 | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hungry** | ~2-3 hr after last feed | 6+ hours no wet diaper / weight not gaining | Feed |
| 2 | **Wet / dirty diaper** | Any time | Diaper is soiled | Change diaper |
| 3 | **Hot / cold** | Any time | Back of neck sweating / cold | Adjust clothing / room temp |
| 4 | **Gas (intestinal / stomach)** | Mostly evening | Knees curled / hard belly | See gas tree |
| 5 | **Colic (3-week colic)** | Evening peak 18-22h, ≥ 3 days/week, ≥ 3 weeks | "Rule of 3" — 3 hrs/day, 3 days/wk, 3 wks | Soothe + wait (self-resolves 3-4 mo) |
| 6 | **Over-tired** | After missing sleep window | Rubbing eyes / yawning | Soothe to sleep quickly |
| 7 | **Want to be held** | Any time | All physical needs OK | Hold (won't spoil 0-3 mo) |

> **快速自查方法**: go through 1→7 in order, **most babies can be soothed within 5 min** by checking these 7

---

## 7 red flags (immediate medical visit)

- 🟥 Crying with **fever ≥ 38°C** (newborn period)
- 🟥 Crying with **vomiting / diarrhea / blood in stool**
- 🟥 Crying with **lethargy / poor feeding / difficult to wake**
- 🟥 Crying with **skin color change** (blue / mottled / severely yellow)
- 🟥 Crying with **difficulty breathing** (nasal flaring / intercostal retractions / rapid breathing)
- 🟥 **High-pitched scream** (different from usual crying, sounds like pain)
- 🟥 **Persistent crying > 3 hours** with no pause (could be intussusception / hair tourniquet / other emergencies)

---

## 5 common myths

- ❌ **"Crying = hungry"** — only one of 7 reasons, **don't feed to shut up every cry**
- ❌ **"Holding spoils the baby"** — **wrong for 0-3 mo**; infants this age **cannot be spoiled**, physical contact is a need
- ❌ **"Let baby cry it out"** — applies to 6+ mo, **not 0-3 mo**; prolonged crying causes stress + worsens gas
- ❌ **"Colic = lactose intolerance"** — most colic is **functional** (no disease), not food allergy
- ❌ **"Crying is good for lung exercise"** — **no evidence**; prolonged crying is harmful, respond promptly

---

## 5-step soothing method (the "5 S's")

1. **Swaddle** — wrap snug (0-2 mo) — reduces startle
2. **Side / stomach position** — on your forearm, not in crib (only for soothing, not for sleep)
3. **Shush** — white noise / shushing sound
4. **Swing** — small amplitude shaking (NOT violent shaking)
5. **Suck** — pacifier / breastfeeding

> **Try all 5 in sequence**, usually 1-2 work. If none work in 10 min, **re-check the 7 causes above** (you may have missed something)

---

## 5 colic-specific tips (the "Rule of 3" baby)

1. **Accept that it's not your fault** — colic is **physiological**, not parenting failure
2. **Don't over-rotate soothing methods** — pick 1-2 and stick with them
3. **Take turns with partner** — colic can last 3 hrs/day, **single-parent burnout is real**
4. **Rest when baby rests** — colic babies don't sleep well, **sleep debt accumulates**
5. **Seek help at 3-month mark** — colic usually self-resolves by 3-4 mo, **if not improving, see doctor**

## 快速自查

- [ ] Crying + 1 of 7 red flags = **see doctor immediately**
- [ ] Crying < 1 hour + 7 cause check = usually find a cause
- [ ] Crying > 3 hours straight = **see doctor same day**
- [ ] Evening peak 3+ days/week + ≥ 3 weeks = likely colic (wait it out)
- [ ] Don't "let cry it out" for 0-3 mo
- [ ] Don't feed to shut up every cry
